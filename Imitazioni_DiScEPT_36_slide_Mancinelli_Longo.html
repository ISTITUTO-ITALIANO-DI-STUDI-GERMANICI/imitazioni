Translation as a form of poetic writing, not an auxiliary literary activity

07 / 36 · OBJECTS OF STUDY

Three kinds of evidence

A translation is not just a source poem paired with a published Italian text

Published Italian versions

The poetic forms Bertolucci decided to publish; bibliographical history and possible later revisions.

Multiple source expressions

Different versions and printings may diverge. An editorial reference does not demonstrate which book Bertolucci used.

Manuscripts and drafts

Material documents can reveal deletions, substitutions and unfinished alternatives—not only final equivalences.

Together: original → translation → revision → poetic writing, with each inference linked to identifiable evidence.

18 / 36 · MODELLING

TEI and ontology do different jobs

Two complementary descriptions of the same research evidence

TEI / XML

Represents what is in the document: verse sequences, word anchors, apparatus readings and manuscript changes.

<l xml:id="..."> · <w> · <app> · <add>
RDF / OWL / PROV

Represents what is connected or asserted: works, translations, alignment evidence, source hypotheses and scholarly responsibility.

dsc:Expression · dsc:TranslationAct
dsc:Alignment · dsc:InterpretiveAssertion
TEI remains authoritative for textual content; RDF links documents, relations, interpretation and provenance. Neither replaces the other.
